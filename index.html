    How it works

    Kodiak looks one move ahead, then two, then three, and keeps going deeper until its time is up (iterative deepening with alpha-beta search). It remembers the best move it found in each position, so every new pass tries the strongest moves first and can skip most of the tree. When the search ends in the middle of a trade, it keeps playing out the captures, so it doesn't misjudge a position halfway through an exchange.

    It scores positions using material, where each piece stands, and some pawn structure. Strength levels just change how long it thinks: Cub stops at 2 moves deep, Grizzly gets about a second, and Kodiak gets about 2.5 seconds. The details are in the README.

    Keys: F flips the board, U undoes a move, H gives a hint. The URL updates as you play, so you can send someone the game.
