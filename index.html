    How it works

    Kodiak is alpha-beta search with iterative deepening. It looks 1 move ahead, then 2, then 3, until its time is up. It remembers the best move from each pass so the next pass can try it first. At the end of the search it keeps playing out captures, so it never stops in the middle of a trade.

    It scores positions by material and where the pieces stand, and also looks at pawn structure. Everything is written from scratch: about 900 lines, with no libraries.
